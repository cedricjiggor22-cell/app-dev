# 🎬 My Favorite Series: **The Lord of the Rings**

## 📖 Overview
**The Lord of the Rings** is set in the fictional world of Middle-earth, the films follow the hobbit **Frodo Baggins** as he and the Company of the Ring embark on a quest to destroy the One Ring to defeat its maker, the Dark Lord Sauron.

---

## 🧪 Main Characters
- **Frodo Baggins** – Frodo is a hobbit of the Shire who inherits the One Ring from his cousin and undertakes the quest to destroy it in the fires of Mount Doom in Mordor.
- **Samwise Gamgee** – Samwise is the chief supporting character of The Lord of the Rings, serving as the loyal companion of the protagonist Frodo Baggins.
- **Aragorn** – Aragorn is a Ranger of the North, first introduced with the name Strider and later revealed to be the heir of Isildur, an ancient King of Arnor and Gondor.
- **Gandalf** – He is a wizard, one of the Istari order, and the leader of the Company of the Ring.

---

## 🌟 Why I Love It
1. Amazing **plot line and destinations**
2. Intense and unpredictable **Fight scenes**
3. Beautiful **cinematography** and **symbolism**
4. Brilliant **acting performances**
